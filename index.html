Perspective

Technology changes. Business fundamentals don’t.

AI, cloud, data, ERP, CRM, automation and whatever comes next can create enormous value. But technology by itself isn’t a strategy.

Successful transformation still depends on leadership, relationships, sound decisions, clear objectives, execution, and getting the right people in the room. That’s where I tend to do my best work.
